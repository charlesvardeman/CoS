# Project Participant Reconciliation

Use this procedure when an operational output depends on who is involved in a project, what role or authority they hold, or who owns the immediate action.

1. Retrieve the project note and its typed person relationships from the vault.
2. Retrieve only the relevant live participation evidence, such as message headers, calendar attendees, or task ownership.
3. Distinguish current event participation, durable project involvement, role or authority, and immediate responsibility.
4. Classify material discrepancies as missing-participant, missing-role, possibly-stale, conflicting, or ambiguous.
5. Use live sources for current event membership and the vault for curated durable context. Do not infer durable role or authority from attendance, authorship, a carbon copy, or one task assignment.
6. If uncertainty changes the output, verify it or use neutral language.
7. When a material semantic gap appears, emit a bounded capture proposal with the project, person, claimed relationship or role, evidence locator, observation time, confidence, and proposed change. Do not write semantic memory without explicit approval.

During a heartbeat, surface the gap only when it affects an imminent meeting, message, commitment, delegation, or decision. Do not turn periodic review into a generic roster-completeness audit.
